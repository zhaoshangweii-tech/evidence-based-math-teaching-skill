# Privacy and Data Boundary

This repository contains generalized teaching-research methods only.

It does not include:

- teacher or student names,
- school or class identifiers,
- student numbers or contact information,
- student work, handwriting, images, recordings, or interview transcripts,
- assessment records or raw research data,
- local file paths,
- private source documents,
- credentials or access tokens.

Generic terms such as "teacher", "student", "sample ID", and "student evidence" describe reusable workflow roles and data-handling principles. They do not refer to identifiable individuals.

Before contributing, remove or replace all identifying details and project-specific examples. Do not commit raw student data or restricted teaching materials.
